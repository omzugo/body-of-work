# Painted Face

A live webcam collage: your face, hands and body, rebuilt from scraps of paintings.

**Open it:** https://omzugo.github.io/body-of-work/ (allow the camera; nothing leaves your browser)

The webcam is tracked in the browser with [ml5.js](https://ml5js.org) (face, hands, body). Each
feature becomes a simple shape (an oval for an eye, a capsule for a finger bone), filled with a
hand-picked scrap of a painting cut to that shape: real eyes and eye-like things for eyes; columns,
branches and legs for limbs. Drawn with [p5.js](https://p5js.org).

Keys: **h** help · **c** credit for the latest painting · **s** show shapes · **v** show webcam ·
**space** new scraps · **f** fullscreen.

Paintings: public-domain works from the [Art Institute of Chicago](https://www.artic.edu/open-access)
open-access collection (CC0).
